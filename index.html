Privacy Policy

App Name: Hisab Soja (হিসাব সোজা) • Effective Date: November 22, 2026

Jihadul Islam ("we," "our," or "us") built the Hisab Soja (হিসাব সোজা) mobile application as an ad-supported tour and group expense management app. This Privacy Policy informs users regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of information when using our application.

Summary: All your tour records, member names, phone numbers, and expense entries are stored locally on your device. We do not collect or upload your personal financial records to any external server.

1. Information Storage and Usage

Hisab Soja is designed with user privacy in mind. When you use the app to create tours, add group members, or track expenses:

  • Local Device Storage: All data you enter—including tour titles, budgets, expense items, member names, and optional phone numbers—is stored exclusively on your device's local database.
  • No External Cloud Sync by Us: We do not operate any backend server that collects, reads, or stores your personal expense logs or contact entries.
  • Backup & Restore: The app provides a manual JSON backup and restore feature. Backup files are saved directly to your chosen device storage location, and you maintain full control over those files.
  • Sharing Reports: When you generate and share expense summaries (as text, image cards, or PDF files), the data is shared only through the external apps you explicitly select (such as WhatsApp, Messenger, or Email).

2. Internet Connectivity & Third-Party Services

While your expense data remains on your device, the app requires an active internet connection to operate and uses third-party services that may collect certain device-level information for advertising and analytics purposes.

Link to the privacy policy of third-party service providers used by the app:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

We use Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements within the app to keep the service free for users. Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain data, including:

  • Device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate general location (derived from IP)
  • Device type, operating system version, and app interaction diagnostics

You can manage or reset your Advertising ID and opt out of personalized advertising at any time through your Android device's Settings > Google > Ads.

4. App Permissions

Hisab Soja may request the following standard permissions solely to support core app functionality:

  • Internet & Network State: Required to verify network connectivity and load advertisements via Google AdMob.
  • Storage / File Access (Scoped): Used only when you export JSON backups, save summary cards/PDF reports, or select a JSON file to restore data.

5. Data Security and Retention

Because your tour and expense records stay locally on your phone, you have complete control over data retention. Uninstalling the app or clearing the app's storage from your Android settings will permanently delete all locally stored records unless you have manually exported a backup file.

6. Children's Privacy

This application is intended for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data through third-party ad networks, please contact us so we can guide you on appropriate steps.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect app improvements or regulatory requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
